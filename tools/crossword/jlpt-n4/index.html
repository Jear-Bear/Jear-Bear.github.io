How it works

  • Pick Beginner: it uses N5 and N4 words.
  • Try the 9×9 daily for longer words and more crossings.
  • Turn on the kanji mode when you want to practise writing the same words in kanji.

Questions

What’s the difference between the mini and the daily?
The mini is a quick 5×5 grid, a couple of minutes. The daily is 9×9 with longer words and takes 5 to 15 minutes.
Is there a streak?
Yes. Solve one daily puzzle each day; like the New York Times crossword, each day’s puzzles count for 48 hours.
