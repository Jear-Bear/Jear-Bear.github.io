Kanji Crossword Puzzles.

In the kanji mode you get a word’s meaning and write it in kanji, one character per square. Words cross where they share a kanji, so a kanji you know in one word helps you find another. It’s recall practice for kanji, the hardest part of Japanese to keep in your head.

Free every day, no account needed.

十字Crossword

How it works

  • Read the meaning, then type the reading with a Japanese keyboard and convert it, or tap the kanji tiles under the grid.
  • Turn on readings in ⚙ for an easier puzzle.
  • Beginner uses N5–N4 words; intermediate and advanced go up to N1.

Questions

Do I need a Japanese keyboard?
No. On a phone or computer, kanji tiles under the grid let you tap the characters in.
Is there a hiragana version?
Yes: the kana mode has the same levels with answers in hiragana.
