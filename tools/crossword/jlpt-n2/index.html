JLPT N2 Crossword Puzzles.

N2 vocabulary is big, abstract and easy to half-know. A crossword won’t let you half-know a word: you have to produce it, kana by kana. The intermediate puzzles mix N3 and N2 words, with fun extras along the way.

How it works

  • Pick Intermediate for N3–N2, or Advanced when you want N1 and slang.
  • Use the kanji mode to practise writing the same words.
  • Solve daily to build a streak; yesterday’s puzzle still counts today.

Questions

How is this different from flashcards?
Flashcards show you the word. A crossword gives you the meaning and makes you recall the word yourself, with crossing letters as hints, which is better practice for real use.
Is it free?
Today’s and yesterday’s puzzles are free, with no account. Crossword+ adds the archive, crosswords from your own Anki deck and bonus puzzles.
