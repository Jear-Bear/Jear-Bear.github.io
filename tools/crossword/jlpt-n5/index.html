JLPT N5 Crossword Puzzles.

Studying for the JLPT N5? Flashcards teach you the words; a crossword makes you pull them out of memory, which is what sticks. Every beginner puzzle is built from the N5 and N4 vocabulary lists, with English clues and hiragana answers.

Play today’s JLPT N5 crossword →

Free every day, no account needed.

十字Crossword

How it works

  • A new beginner mini (5×5) and daily (9×9) every day, at your midnight.
  • Clues are in English; answers are N5–N4 words in hiragana.
  • After you solve, every answer is listed with its kanji and meaning, so each puzzle doubles as a short vocab review.

Questions

Is it only N5 words?
Beginner puzzles mix N5 and N4 words, with a few fun extras like food and pop culture. That keeps them solvable for N5 learners and useful on the way to N4.
Can I play older puzzles?
Today’s and yesterday’s puzzles are free. The whole archive is part of Crossword+.
