JLPT N3 Crossword Puzzles.

Intermediate puzzles are built from the N3 and N2 lists: the words that show up in news, manga and everyday conversation once you’re past the basics. They’re a good check of how much of your intermediate vocabulary you can actually recall.

Free every day, no account needed.

十字Crossword

How it works

  • Pick Intermediate: N3 and N2 words, English clues.
  • Stuck? Check a word or reveal a square; your time still counts, marked as helped.
  • Every answer is listed after you solve, with kanji and meaning.

Questions

Are the clues in English?
Yes, for beginner, intermediate and advanced. The Mixed level has clues in Japanese, like a newspaper crossword.
Can I use a Japanese keyboard?
Yes. Type in hiragana and press Enter, without converting to kanji, or type romaji with the IME off.
