JLPT N1 Crossword Puzzles.

Advanced puzzles use N1 vocabulary plus the slang, idioms and set phrases you only meet in native material. If the intermediate level feels easy, this is where it stops being easy.

How it works

  • Pick Advanced: N1 words, idioms and slang, English clues.
  • Or pick Mixed for words from every level with clues written in Japanese.
  • Your best time and streak are kept for each level.

Questions

Is there anything harder?
Try the kanji mode at the advanced level: you write each answer in kanji, one character per square, from its meaning.
Can I play on my phone?
Yes. On a phone the puzzle has its own kana keyboard under the grid.
