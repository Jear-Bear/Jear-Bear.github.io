Hiragana Crossword Puzzles.

Know your hiragana and want to actually use it? These crosswords give you an English clue and you fill in the answer in hiragana, one kana per square. The words come from the first JLPT levels, so you’ll recognise most of them, and the crossing words help when you’re stuck.

Free every day, no account needed.

十字Crossword

How it works

  • Read the English clue, then type the answer in romaji (it turns into kana as you go) or with a Japanese keyboard.
  • Small kana are written full size, like in a Japanese newspaper: きゃ becomes きや, がっこう becomes がつこう.
  • Start with the 5×5 mini. When it feels easy, try the 9×9 daily at the same level.

Questions

Do I need to know kanji?
No. In kana mode every answer is written in hiragana, and the clues are in English.
How hard is it?
Beginner puzzles use JLPT N5 and N4 words: everyday things like food, family, time and places. Intermediate and advanced levels are there when you want more.
