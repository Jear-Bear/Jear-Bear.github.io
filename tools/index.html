Free Japanese practice.

Games and trainers to practice Japanese every day, for free: a daily Japanese crossword, Japanese Wordle in kana, and drills for hiragana, katakana, kanji and typing. Made by someone who actually uses them. No account needed.

ツールTools

  • 十 Japanese Crossword A free Japanese crossword every day. Kana mode has English clues and hiragana answers; kanji mode has you write each word in kanji. A mini and a 9×9 daily at JLPT N5–N4, N3–N2 or N1 level. dailykanakanjiJLPT Open →
  • 語 Japanese Wordle (Kana Wordle) Wordle in Japanese: guess the hidden word in hiragana in 8 tries. A new 4- and 5-kana word every day at three JLPT levels, unlimited practice, and a blue hint when you’re one dakuten away. dailygamevocabulary Open →
  • あ Kana Trainer: Hiragana and Katakana Practice Learn and practice hiragana and katakana with fast, adaptive drills and spaced repetition. Tracks your trouble pairs (シ↔ツ, ぬ↔め…) and lets you test into your level. hiraganakatakanaSRS Open →
  • 漢 Kanji Trainer Kanji practice by jōyō, jinmeiyō, JLPT, Kanken or your own list (paste, CSV or Excel). Meanings, readings, common words and handwriting with stroke-order checking, on a spaced-repetition schedule. kanjiwritingJLPTKanken Open →
  • 難 Nandoku Trainer Every level of 漢字でGO!, 1 through 8, plus the 別表記 spellings: 10,000+ hard-to-read words shown the way the game shows them. Type the reading, pick the meaning, or play a timed challenge run. kanjireadings漢字でGO! Open →
  • ぬ JIS Grind Learn Japanese kana typing on a JIS keyboard. A kana appears, you hit the matching physical key (detected by position, so your IME stays put). Weak kana come up more often. JIStypingkana Open →

Where to start

Every tool here is free, works in your browser on a phone or a computer, and needs no account. A good daily routine is a few minutes of drills, then a game at your level.

Just starting Japanese
Learn hiragana and katakana with the Kana Trainer, then try the beginner Japanese Wordle to read real words in kana.
Beginner (JLPT N5–N4)
Do the beginner mini Japanese crossword every day, and start on the N5 kanji in the Kanji Trainer.
Intermediate (JLPT N3–N2)
Switch the crossword and Wordle to intermediate, try the crossword’s kanji mode, and practice writing kanji by JLPT level.
Advanced (N1 and beyond)
Advanced crosswords mix in slang and idioms, the Mixed crossword has clues in Japanese, and the Nandoku Trainer covers the hardest readings from 漢字でGO!.
