Privacy.

What the site collects, why, and how to see it or have it deleted. Short answer: as little as possible, never sold.

個人情報Privacy

Last updated October 11, 2026

The short version

  • No ads trackers, no cookies for tracking, and I never sell your data.
  • If you use Crossword+, I keep your email, your membership status and your crossword progress, so it works on all your devices.
  • Payments go through Stripe; I never see your card number.
  • Email support@jareddesu.com to see or delete your data.

1. Who’s responsible

jareddesu.com is run by me, Jared Perlmutter (“Jared”, “I”, “me”). I’m responsible for the personal data described here. Contact: support@jareddesu.com.

3. Who I share it with

Only the services that run the site, each only for its job:

  • Stripe: payments and subscriptions. Stripe handles your card details; I never see them. Stripe’s privacy policy.
  • Cloudflare: hosts the account server and database, and provides the cookieless analytics.
  • GitHub Pages: hosts the website itself.
  • Resend: sends the sign-in links and the welcome email.
  • Google: only if you choose “Sign in with Google”.
  • Web3Forms: delivers contact and sponsorship form messages to my inbox.

I don’t sell or rent your data, and I don’t share it with advertisers. Sponsors only ever get totals (like “120 clicks this week”), never anything about you.

4. Cookies and storage

The site doesn’t use tracking or advertising cookies. It stores a few things in your browser’s local storage so the site works: your settings, your crossword progress, your imported deck, and, if you’re signed in, your session.

5. Your choices and rights

  • See or delete your data: email support@jareddesu.com from the address on your account. I’ll send you a copy, or delete your account and progress, usually within a few days. Payment records Stripe must keep for tax reasons stay with Stripe.
  • Sign out everywhere: “Sign out” on the crossword page ends your session on that device; email me if you want every device signed out.
  • Waitlist: every waitlist email included a link to remove yourself in one click.
  • Depending on where you live (for example the EU, UK or California), you may have further rights, such as correcting your data, objecting to its use, or complaining to your data protection authority. Email me and I’ll help.

6. Security

Data travels over HTTPS. Sign-in uses one-time links or Google instead of passwords, and sessions can be ended from my side at any time. No system is perfect, but I keep what I collect small, which keeps the risk small.

7. Where data is stored

The services above may store data in the United States and other countries. When data leaves your country, it’s protected by those companies’ standard safeguards.

8. Children

The site isn’t directed at children under 13, and I don’t knowingly collect their data. If you think a child has signed up, email me and I’ll delete the account.

9. Changes

If this policy changes, the date at the top changes too. For important changes, I’ll email Crossword+ members.
