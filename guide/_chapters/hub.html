読How to read this / 使い方

The chapters go roughly in the order you’ll need them, from your very first kana to keeping yourself going years in. Total beginner? Start at chapter 0 and go in order. Already immersing? Jump to whatever’s bugging you; every chapter stands on its own.

There’s stuff to poke at in every chapter: calculators, little games, a pitch-accent viewer, a conversation simulator. Your progress, checklists, and the “why” you pin in chapter 1 are saved in your browser. No account, no tracking, no course to sell you at the end. Knowledge is free.
