読How to read this / 使い方

I started Japanese about eight years ago, spent six of them on textbooks and classes, quit four or five times, and then restarted with immersion two years ago. I’m a little over 4,000 hours in, reading most days, and taking the N1 this December. Still まだまだ. This guide is basically everything I’ve said in my videos, cleaned up and put in order.

The chapters go roughly in the order you’ll need them, from your first kana to keeping yourself going years in. If you’re a total beginner, start at chapter 1. If you’re already immersing, jump to whatever’s bugging you. There are a few things to click on along the way, and your progress is saved in your browser. No account, and nothing to buy at the end.
