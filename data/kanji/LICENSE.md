# Kanji Trainer data — sources and license

The files in this folder are generated by `scripts/kanji/build-data.py` from:

- **Kanjium** by Uros O. — https://github.com/mifunetoshiro/kanjium
  (CC BY-SA 4.0). Kanji readings, meanings, stroke counts, school grades,
  Kanken levels, look-alike kanji and example words. Kanjium is built on
  **KANJIDIC** and **EDICT/JMdict** by the Electronic Dictionary Research and
  Development Group (https://www.edrdg.org/), used under the Group's licence
  (CC BY-SA 4.0).
- **KanjiVG** by Ulrich Apel — https://kanjivg.tagaini.net (CC BY-SA 3.0).
  Stroke-order paths.
- **kanji-data** by David Gouveia — https://github.com/davidluzgouveia/kanji-data
  (MIT). Jinmeiyō list (from KANJIDIC2) and JLPT levels (from Jonathan Waller's
  lists at http://www.tanos.co.uk/; there are no official JLPT lists since 2010).

The derived data in `data/kanji/` is shared under **CC BY-SA 4.0**
(https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/4.0/). The trainer's code is
covered by the repository's own license.

Notes:
- Kanken 10級–2級 follow the jōyō school-grade split (2020 allocation).
  準1級 and 1級 lists are approximate: the Kanken association doesn't publish
  closed lists for them.
- The official jōyō list prints 𠮟 and 剝; the trainer uses the everyday
  forms 叱 and 剥 (both are allowed) with the official readings.
