# Nandoku Trainer data — sources and licenses

Everything here is generated by `scripts/nandoku/build-data.py`.

## Words (`terms.json`)

Words, readings, meanings, notes, other spellings (別表記), answer-length hints
(文字指定) and categories for levels 1–8 and こころのリテラシー of **漢字でGO!**
(https://plicy.net/GamePlay/155561), from the fan wiki **漢字でGO！ 問題集 @wiki**
(https://w.atwiki.jp/yuia_sk/), parsed by `scripts/nandoku/parse-wiki.py`. Levels
1–3 are partial because the wiki hasn't transcribed all of them yet. For words the
wiki shares with **kanjidego-yomitan-anki** by MarvNC
(https://github.com/MarvNC/kanjidego-yomitan-anki), that project's other
spellings, alternate answers (別解) and notes are merged in.

Neither source states a license; the data is used here with credit, for an
unofficial, non-commercial fan study tool. 漢字でGO! and its content belong to
their creators.

Changes: words the game shows only as images (characters Unicode doesn't have)
and questions the wiki marks as no longer shown in the game are left out; wiki
typos in readings (stray brackets or letters) are cleaned. The 別表記 set turns
each other spelling into its own question with the readings and meaning of the
word it spells (spellings that are descriptions, like `⿰虫𢏣`, are left out).

## Font (`fonts/`)

"Nandoku Pop" is a derived web font, subset to the characters used and split
into chunks. The game itself uses the commercial fonts セイビタカナワB and
わんぱくルイカ, which can't be redistributed; this is a free lookalike.

- **Mochiy Pop One** by the Mochiypop Project Authors
  (https://github.com/fontdasu/Mochiypop), **SIL Open Font License 1.1**,
  included as `fonts/OFL-MochiyPopOne.txt`. Used for kana and most kanji.
- **IPAmj Mincho** (IPAmj明朝) Ver.003.01 by the Information-technology Promotion
  Agency, Japan, now distributed by the Character Information Technology Promotion
  Council: https://moji.or.jp/mojikiban/font/. Used under the **IPA Font License
  Agreement v1.0**, included as `fonts/IPA_Font_License_Agreement_v1.0.txt`, for
  rare kanji Mochiy Pop One lacks. Their outlines are thickened
  (`scripts/nandoku/embolden.py`) to match; the derived glyphs are distributed
  under the same license. To use the original instead, download IPAmj Mincho from
  the link above and point `--ipamj` in the build script at it.
- **Jigmo** by Koichi Kamichi (https://kamichikoichi.github.io/jigmo/),
  **CC0 1.0**, thickened the same way, for characters neither covers (mostly
  CJK Extensions G and H).
