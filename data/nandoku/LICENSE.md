# Nandoku Trainer data — sources and licenses

Everything here is generated by `scripts/nandoku/build-data.py`.

## Words (`terms.json`)

Words, readings, alternate answers (別解), other spellings (別表記), meanings (意味)
and notes (追記) for levels 5–7 of **漢字でGO!** (https://plicy.net/GamePlay/155561),
from **kanjidego-yomitan-anki** by MarvNC
(https://github.com/MarvNC/kanjidego-yomitan-anki). That project compiled them
from the game and the fan wiki 漢字でGO! 問題集 @wiki (https://w.atwiki.jp/kanjidego/).
The source repository doesn't state a license; the data is used here with credit,
for an unofficial, non-commercial fan study tool. 漢字でGO! and its content belong
to their creators.

Changes: four readings with a scraping glitch were corrected
(躪り書き, 鬨の声, 犂牛の喩え, 亥豕の譌), 24 words the game shows only as images
(characters Unicode doesn't have) were left out, and placeholder spellings
such as `<6文字指定>` were dropped.

## Font (`fonts/`)

"Nandoku Mincho" is a derived font made by subsetting and splitting:

- **IPAmj Mincho** (IPAmj明朝) Ver.003.01 by the Information-technology Promotion
  Agency, Japan, now distributed by the Character Information Technology Promotion
  Council: https://moji.or.jp/mojikiban/font/. Used under the **IPA Font License
  Agreement v1.0**, included as `fonts/IPA_Font_License_Agreement_v1.0.txt`;
  the derived font is distributed under the same license. To swap in the original,
  download IPAmj Mincho from the link above and point `--ipamj` in the build
  script at it (or use it directly in `fonts/fonts.css`).
- **Jigmo** by Koichi Kamichi (https://kamichikoichi.github.io/jigmo/),
  **CC0 1.0**, for the characters IPAmj Mincho doesn't cover (mostly CJK
  Extensions G and H).
